# Vinco 1.0.0

The first version: everything built up to 12 October 2026 (Steps 1 to 13 and Wave 2), recorded as the baseline every later version is compared with. Everything runs on the phone; there is no account and no server. Test APKs only, not yet on the Play Store.

## Release notes

Vinco turns a 30, 60 or 90 day plan into a campaign you can win. Every day, hit your four orders (water, wake-up, a protein meal, a workout), take a selfie as proof, and seal the day. Miss one and a Truce (rest day) can save your run. Earn ranks, decorations and denarii (coins) to spend in the Forum (shop). Or train freely with no streak at stake. Three voices: calm, firm or savage. Your selfies never leave your phone.

## Features

### Onboarding

- Splash, then **Cross the Rubicon** (Begin your arc) under rotating Stoic quotes.
- **Arc length:** a 30, 60 or 90 day campaign, or **Free training** (no end, no streak).
- **Your orders:** Vinco's four (water, wake-up, a protein meal, a workout), each with a minimum ("Hold the line") and a full goal ("Conquer") set with steppers, plus the wake time.
- **Tone:** Philosopher (calm), Centurion (firm) or Roast me (savage), with a live preview.
- **The oath:** a 20-second voice note on why, sealed with wax; heard again from a set day of the arc.
- **First selfie** with the DAY I stamp.
- Leaving mid-way resumes at the same step.

### Today (Veni)

- Header "DAY XII OF LX", chips for the campaign, Truces, rank and denarii, and a line in the chosen tone that follows the day's progress.
- **Dawn card:** "I'm up" opens the wake sheet (woke at, went to sleep at, hours slept); the orders open after it.
- **Orders:** water and meals by tap, workout by sheet (minimum, full goal or exact minutes), amounts past the goal counted up to a cap. Tap to log, press and hold to take one back. A progress ring of orders held.
- **Own orders:** up to 4, each with a name, unit, minimum and full goal, logged in two levels.
- **Sealing:** every order at its minimum holds the day ("Line held" seal card); every order at its full goal conquers it, and the VINCO stamp slams down.
- **Day card** to share: the day's real numbers in three styles.
- **Proof tiles:** the daily selfie (yesterday's photo as a ghost outline, a review step), body weight, and the timelapse.
- **Sick day:** call a Truce in advance from the dawn card; it comes back if the day is held anyway.
- **To-do:** every-day and today tasks inline; one "How often?" sheet (just today, tomorrow, every day, or make it an order); leftovers from earlier days carried over or dropped from a card.

### The campaign

- Days are sealed at midnight as conquered, held or missed, and never change after.
- **Truces** (rest days) are called by the user: one when an arc begins, one per 7 days of campaign, at most 3 held, or bought with denarii.
- A broken campaign shows the campaign-lost screen once, then **Resurgo** (I rise again) starts Day I again.
- A finished campaign offers another campaign or free training.
- **Free training:** the same orders, no streak, no loss screen, no Truces; a "days trained" chip.

### Vidi (Progress)

- Stats, a month calendar of conquered, held, missed and Truce days.
- **Commentarii** (logs): weekly bars for sleep, water and workout, a weight line, an insight per week.
- The timelapse of the arc's selfies.

### Vici (Arc)

- The arc's journey line, rank card (eight ranks, Tiro to Caesar), record (current and best campaign, days conquered and held, Truces used, selfies).
- Treasury (denarii, Truces, alarm passes), decorations (earned and locked with how to earn), every selfie across arcs, past arcs.
- Start a campaign from free training, switch to free training, or sound the retreat (reset, confirmed by typing RETREAT).

### Castra (Settings tab)

- Tone, appearance (Basalt dark, Marble light, or the phone's setting), sounds, vibration, selfie outline strength.
- Orders and to-dos (`/plan`), wake-up time, the oath, Guard levels and Aurora in preview, retreat, version, dev mode in dev builds.

### The Forum (shop) and the Aerarium (ways to earn)

- Denarii are earned (a day held, a day conquered, every 7 days of campaign), never bought with money.
- Buy a Truce, an alarm pass ("Venia"), day card frames and app icons. The Aerarium lists every way to earn; history shows the ledger.

### Guard levels and Aurora (preview)

- Pax, Vigil, Custos and Imperium, and the Aurora alarm with its missions, can be chosen and saved now; marked "Arrives with the Vinco build" until the native parts ship.

### Feel

- 17 sounds from the prototype and matching haptics, on by default; the DAY I seal and the VINCO stamp always play. A whisper-quiet tab sound.
- Layouts that stack on narrow phones; the phone's reduce-motion setting respected.
