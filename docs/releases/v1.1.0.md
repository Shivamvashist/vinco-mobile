# Vinco 1.1.0

Wave 3 (plan and decisions: [TASKS-2026-10-13.md](../TASKS-2026-10-13.md)). A minor version: new features and changed flows, nothing a user loses on update.

## Release notes

- Start from your own daily orders, or pick a ready-made 30, 60 or 90 day campaign.
- A cleaner Today: log your selfie, weight and calories in one tap, and finished orders move down.
- Settings, permissions and your oath now live in the menu at the top of Today.
- Unfinished to-dos move to the next day by themselves.
- Progress: calories next to your weight, and tap any day to see how it went.
- Fits smaller phones better. Sounds are off by default.

## Added

- **Onboarding, "Your daily orders"** (`/onboarding/plan`): water and workout suggested, a + for your own (what, unit, how many a day), a line that orders are done every day. Continue starts free training with exactly those orders (none is allowed).
- **User menu** on Today: account (coming), Settings, Orders and to-dos, Permissions, Guard, Aurora, the Forum, and the oath.
- **Permissions screen** (`/permissions`): camera and microphone with live status and a way to the phone's settings.
- **Quick logs** on Today: selfie, weight and calories; each stands out until done today, then shrinks to a ticked circle.
- **Calories:** whole kcal for the day, optional, from the quick log.
- **No orders yet** card in free training, suggesting water in one tap.
- **Run nudge** in free training: a "3-day run" chip and "Finish today's orders to keep your 3-day run going."
- **Day summary:** tap any calendar day in Vidi for its orders against goals, sleep, weight, calories, selfie and to-dos.
- **Body chart** in Vidi: weight and calories on one date line, each on its own scale, each can be hidden (remembered).
- **Typed amounts** for own orders over 20 a day (steps): a tap opens a sheet to type the amount.
- **Sizes follow the phone's width:** narrow phones get the same layout a little smaller; touch targets never shrink.

## Changed

| Flow                       | Was (1.0.0)                                                         | Now (1.1.0)                                                                                            |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Onboarding order           | Rubicon, arc length, Vinco's four, tone, oath, selfie (6 steps)     | Rubicon, your daily orders, tone, oath, selfie (4 steps); or a ready-made campaign (6 steps)           |
| Free training orders       | Always Vinco's four                                                 | Only the orders picked; Vinco's can be removed and added back in Orders and to-dos                     |
| A day with no orders       | Not possible                                                        | Never held, never stamped                                                                              |
| Own orders                 | Name, unit, minimum, full goal; tap moves up a level; up to 4       | What, unit, how many (minimum = goal); a tap adds one unit, up to 20 a day; up to 6                    |
| Today layout               | Tone line, progress ring, proof tiles, rank chip                    | User button, chips, quick logs, orders with done ones sinking ("3 of 5 done"), to-dos                  |
| Rows                       | Taller order rows                                                   | Shorter rows; a done row is muted                                                                      |
| To-dos left undone         | A card asked to carry over or drop                                  | Moved to today by themselves, marked "carried over"                                                    |
| Settings                   | A fourth tab, Castra                                                | A screen from the user menu; three tabs (Veni, Vidi, Vici)                                             |
| The oath                   | Heard from a set day of the arc, in Settings                        | Heard twice a day from Day I, in the user menu; the question looks three months ahead in free training |
| Weight in Vidi             | A weight line                                                       | The body chart with calories                                                                           |
| Missed days, free training | Shown as missed                                                     | Shown as days off                                                                                      |
| Timelapse                  | From Today's proof section and Vidi                                 | From Vidi                                                                                              |
| Sounds                     | On by default; seal and stamp always on                             | Off by default; the seal, the oath locking in and the stamp always play                                |
| Sizes and text             | Fixed sizes; text grew with the phone's font size up to higher caps | Trimmed sizes scaled to the screen; lower caps on text growth                                          |

## Removed

- The tone quote line, the progress ring, the proof tiles and the rank chip on Today.
- The carry-over card.
- The Settings (Castra) tab.
- "Free training" as a card on the arc length step (it is now the Continue button on "Your daily orders").

## Data and upgrade

- Migration `0009_day_calories`: `day_logs.calories` (nullable).
- Arcs keep the Vinco orders they have rows for; existing campaigns keep all four. Free training arcs made before 1.1.0 keep all four until the user removes some.
- Own orders made before 1.1.0 keep their separate minimum ("minimum held" shows once it's reached) and now log by count like new ones; goals over 20 open the typed amount.
- Saved settings: `hiddenBodyLines` added to preferences; the onboarding draft moves to version 2 (`picks`, `ownOrders`; a half-finished onboarding resumes safely); the oath plays per day are counted.
- Sounds off applies to new installs only; an existing install keeps its sound setting.

## Not yet

- Local reminders (`expo-notifications`, works in Expo Go), then the oath as a twice-a-day reminder.
- The day summary judges past days by the arc's current goals.
- Account and sign-in (the menu shows it as coming).
