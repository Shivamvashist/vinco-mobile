# Vinco 1.1.0

Wave 3 (plan and decisions: [TASKS-2026-10-13.md](../TASKS-2026-10-13.md)) and Wave 4 ([TASKS-2026-10-31.md](../TASKS-2026-10-31.md)). A minor version: new features and changed flows, nothing a user loses on update. Nothing had shipped when Wave 4 began, so it joined this version.

## Release notes

- Start from your own daily orders, or a ready-made 30, 60 or 90 day campaign.
- A new Today: your day at a glance, quick logs, plus and minus on orders.
- Earn denarii the moment your day holds, plus run pay for days in a row.
- Aurora, the alarm that stops only once you are up.
- Focus mode: pick a time and a guard, earn denarii when you hold it.
- New palettes, day card designs and reminders.
- Edit any order; see a chart for each one in Progress.
- A better fit on small phones; sounds off by default.

## Added

### Wave 4 (31 Oct)

- **Live denarii:** the day pays the moment it holds (5 held, 10 conquered) and the pay is taken back if an undo, an edit or a new order stops it holding; the midnight seal makes it final (it replaces the live rows, never adds). A short toast says "+15 denarii" or that they were taken back.
- **Stipendium (run pay):** days 3, 5 and 7 of every week of a run pay 10, 15 and 25; runs of 14, 30, 60 and 100 days pay 50, 100, 200 and 300. In a campaign every 7th day also grants a Truce (at the seal). A card under the orders shows this week of the run, today's pay and the next reward.
- **Order rows:** orders with levels (water, meals, own counted orders) show a minus and a plus on the right; done-or-not orders toggle with the check circle (or a tap on the row). Screen readers get increment and decrement, or a checkbox.
- **Order charts:** an "Orders" log in Vidi with a week chart for every order (bars against the goal, or ticks for done-or-not), each shown or hidden with its chip (remembered).
- **Palettes:** Appearance is a screen (`/appearance`): the mode, then a dark palette (Basalt, Nox, Purpura, Aes) and a light palette (Marble, Papyrus, Calx), each previewed in its own colours. "Match phone" switches between the two picks.
- **Day card designs:** Classic, Numeral, Laurel, Sunrise and Tablet free; Stamp and Memento in the Forum (150 denarii each). Pick a design, then a colour; both are remembered. Memento's line follows the tone and the result.
- **Reminders** (local, [NOTIFICATIONS.md](../NOTIFICATIONS.md)): today's orders (wake time + 30 min, if nothing is logged), seal the day (evening time, default 8:30 pm, with orders left and any run reward), last call (10:45 pm, campaigns of 2+ days), the Truce window (the morning after a miss). At most three a day, none from 11 pm to wake-up, all three tones, withdrawn once the day holds, none on a sick day. Settings, Reminders: a master switch, each kind, the evening time. Tapping one opens Today.
- **Push token:** fetched and kept on the phone in a development or production build, for admin broadcasts later. Not sent anywhere yet.
- **Dev tools:** "Add 100 denarii" (ledger reason `dev_grant`, dev builds only).

### Today and Focus (31 Oct, [TODAY-AND-FOCUS.md](../TODAY-AND-FOCUS.md))

- **The Dies card** (Today's hero): orders held as one big rolling number, a state pill (open, held, conquered, sick day, before dawn, arc complete), the day as the sun's path from wake-up to midnight with a dot at now and a notch per order done, lit from below in the palette's accent (brighter as the day holds). Tap: today's summary.
- **Run and Purse tiles** under it, each with its own glow: the run with this week's seven steps (tap: what the run pays), the purse with today's pay (tap: the Forum).
- **Focus ("Acies")**: a row under the tiles springs open (from its own frame) into the focus view: 5 to 180 minutes on a snapping ruler, a guard (Custos limits apps, Imperium blocks apps, Silentium blocks everything), the reward up front. A countdown ring with rolling digits; "Retreat" needs a 1.5 s hold and pays nothing. Pays 0.2, 0.3 or 0.4 denarii a minute (10 minutes or more, at most 120 counted, three paid focuses a day). "Focus held" notification at the end; a focus that ends while the app is closed shows its completion next time. Blocking arrives with the Vigil; the timer and reward work now.
- **Spring motion:** `motion.spring` tokens and `PressableScale` (presses give and spring back) on buttons, order rows, quick logs, chips, option cards, the hero and tiles; numbers roll (`RollingNumber`); continuous corners on iOS.

### Wave 3 (13 to 17 Oct)

- **Onboarding, "Your daily orders"** (`/onboarding/plan`): water and workout suggested, a + for your own (what, unit, how many a day), a line that orders are done every day. Continue starts free training with exactly those orders (none is allowed).
- **User menu** on Today: account (coming), Settings, Orders and to-dos, Permissions, Guard, Aurora, the Forum, and the oath.
- **Permissions screen** (`/permissions`): camera and microphone with live status and a way to the phone's settings.
- **Quick logs** on Today: selfie, weight and calories; each stands out until done today, then shrinks to a ticked circle.
- **Calories:** whole kcal for the day, optional, from the quick log.
- **No orders yet** card in free training, suggesting water in one tap.
- **Run nudge** in free training: a "3-day run" chip and "Finish today's orders to keep your 3-day run going."
- **Day summary:** tap any calendar day in Vidi for its orders against goals, sleep, weight, calories, selfie and to-dos.
- **Body chart** in Vidi: weight and calories on one date line, each on its own scale, each can be hidden (remembered).
- **Typed amounts** for own orders over 20 a day (steps): a tap opens a sheet to type the amount.
- **Sizes follow the phone's width:** narrow phones get the same layout a little smaller; touch targets never shrink.
- **Edit any order** in Orders and to-dos (17 Oct): your own orders (name, unit, how many, or stand down) and Vinco's full goals and wake time. A new name, unit or a bigger number counts from today; a lower number counts from tomorrow, so it can never rescue the day in progress.
- **Done-or-not orders** (17 Oct): unit and number are optional; leave both empty and the order is simply done or not, once a day.
- **Tasks show an edit pencil** in Orders and to-dos (remove is inside the edit sheet).

## Fixed

- **Free training days sealed against all four orders** (found 17 Oct): the midnight seal didn't pass the arc's kind, so a free-training day was judged on water, wake-up, meals and workout even when only some were picked, and sealed as missed. Days already sealed this way stay as they are (sealed days never change).
- **Day card in free training** listed Vinco's four empty orders; it now shows only the orders the arc has.
- **Selfie retake could blank the screen** on some phones: every shot is now shrunk to 1080 px wide before review (a full camera shot is 3000 px or more, and several at once could run the phone out of memory), the camera stays running behind the check so "Retake" doesn't restart it, and the shutter waits until the camera is ready.

## Removed

- The tone quote line, the progress ring, the proof tiles and the rank chip on Today.
- The carry-over card.
- The Settings (Castra) tab.
- "Free training" as a card on the arc length step (it is now the Continue button on "Your daily orders").

## Data and upgrade

- Migration `0011_focus_sessions` (table `focus_sessions`); focus pay is ledger rows `focus_1` to `focus_3`; reset journey deletes focus sessions.
- **Wave 4 otherwise, no new migration.** Ledger reasons added: `streak_day3`, `streak_day5`, `streak_day7`, `streak_milestone`, `aurora_first_try`, `dev_grant`. Old `campaign_week` rows stay as they were.
- **Saved settings:** preferences gain `darkPaletteId`, `lightPaletteId` and `hiddenOrderCharts` (checked on restore); new stores `vinco.dayCard` and `vinco.reminders`. Forum item ids `designStamp`, `designMemento`.
- An existing install's sealed days keep the pay they had; run pay starts from the next held day.

- Migration `0009_day_calories`: `day_logs.calories` (nullable).
- Arcs keep the Vinco orders they have rows for; existing campaigns keep all four. Free training arcs made before 1.1.0 keep all four until the user removes some.
- Own orders made before 1.1.0 keep their separate minimum ("minimum held" shows once it's reached) and now log by count like new ones; goals over 20 open the typed amount.
- Saved settings: `hiddenBodyLines` added to preferences; the onboarding draft moves to version 2 (`picks`, `ownOrders`; a half-finished onboarding resumes safely); the oath plays per day are counted.
- Sounds off applies to new installs only; an existing install keeps its sound setting.
- Migration `0010_order_replaces`: `custom_orders.replaces_id` (nullable), linking a lower goal waiting for tomorrow to the order it carries on.
- Selfies taken from 1.1.0 are about a tenth of the size; older ones stay as they were.
